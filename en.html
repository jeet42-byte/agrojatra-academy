Inter Services Selection Board

The stages of ISSB

ISSB observes you over several days. We practise each stage separately.

  1. Screening and IQ tests

    Verbal and non-verbal IQ, practised against the clock.

  2. Psychology tests

    WAT, SCT, picture story writing and self description.

  3. GTO tasks

    Group discussion, PGT, HGT, command task, lecturette.

  4. Interview

    A personal interview with the board, practised in mock interviews.

  5. Conference

    The final decision, based on all the assessments.

Frequently asked questions

When are classes held?

Saturday to Thursday between 8:00 and 10:15 pm, with a model test on Friday at 10:00 am. See the routine above.

Can I join classes on my phone?

Yes. All you need is the Zoom or Google Meet app and an internet connection.

No. Selection is decided only by the relevant force or organisation. We help you prepare. Anyone who promises a job in exchange for money is running a scam.
